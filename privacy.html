Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the details you give us when you register your interest or contact us on this website.

Who we are

Seagrape Rentals LLC ("Seagrape", "we", "us") operates seagraperentals.com and is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us using our contact form.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Whether you are planning a stay or own a property, and, if you choose to tell us, the area your property is in.
  • The date and time you gave consent and confirmed your email address.

If you use our contact form, we also receive your name, email address and message.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website, and we do not track you across other websites.

Why we use it

We use these details only to tell you about the launch of Seagrape Rentals and, if you are a property owner, about our management services. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form and confirming your email address.

If you contact us, we use your details only to reply to you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry. Contacting us does not add you to our mailing list.

Who helps us

We use a small number of service providers, who process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Brevo stores our mailing list, sends our emails and delivers contact-form messages to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare receives the form and checks that it was sent by a person rather than an automated program (Cloudflare Turnstile). This check reads technical information about your browser and connection.
  • GitHub hosts this website and, like any web host, records technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs.

These providers may process data outside the Cayman Islands, including in the European Union and the United States. We do not sell your personal data or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. If you never confirm your email address, we delete the unconfirmed sign-up. We keep contact-form messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, and for no more than 12 months.

Your rights

Under the Cayman Islands Data Protection Act (2021 Revision) you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or you can use our contact form. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Office of the Ombudsman in the Cayman Islands (ombudsman.ky).

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
